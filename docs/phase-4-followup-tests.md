## Not Covered

The following capabilities were intentionally excluded or remain pending further operational phase setup:

1. **Persistent Hazard Zone Entity CRUD:**
   Hazard zones are currently accepted exclusively as request-scoped parameters (`blockedZones`) on `POST /assignments/propose`. There is no persistent database entity or dedicated CRUD API (`/zones`), which is deferred to future operational operational phases.
2. **Empirical Telemetry Calibration of Uncertainty Parameters:**
   The coefficients for base variance, water surge, caution impedance, water depth, and weather in `calculateCoefficientOfVariation` and `RiskAwareTravelTimeProvider` are heuristic placeholders (`// placeholder, not measured`) and have not been calibrated against real-world GPS telemetry from active flood rescue missions.
